How It Works

Step 1: Capture Image

The camera continuously captures the surroundings and feeds each frame into OpenCV for real-time processing, preparing the image for object detection and analysis.

Step 2: Object Detection

YOLOv10 detects and categorizes the objects within the image, along with coordinates for bounding boxes and confidence scores. These results are used to calculate each object's direction, estimated distance, size, and color, and to warn about approaching vehicles.

Step 3: Scene Analysis

Gemini describes the scene from the camera image, and GPT combines those descriptions with the detection results into a short summary focused on what matters most, starting with anything safety-related.

Step 4: Audio Feedback

Using pyttsx3 text-to-speech, visual information is converted into spoken audio. Hazard warnings are always spoken first, followed by answers to the user's questions and regular scene updates.

Instructions for VisuAI

Introduction

VisuAI helps visually impaired users understand what is happening around them using object detection, scene description, and voice recognition. It describes your surroundings, answers your questions, warns you about approaching vehicles, and can alert an emergency contact.

Getting Started

Open the Application:

Launch the VisuAI application on your device.

Access the Camera:

The application will open the camera interface, enabling you to capture real-time images.

Wake Word Activation:

To activate the assistant, say the wake word, "Hey Vision," followed by your request, or pause after "Hey Vision" and VisuAI will say "I'm listening." Ensure the microphone is enabled and working for optimal recognition.

Using Object Detection

Capture an Image:

Point the camera at the object or scene you want to analyze. The application automatically detects objects within the camera’s view.

Get Object Information:

Once an object is detected, VisuAI will provide information about it, including:

  • The object's name (e.g., "person," "car," "chair").
  • Its direction as a clock position (e.g., "12 o'clock" is straight ahead).
  • Its size and color (e.g., "large blue").
  • Its estimated distance from you (e.g., "about 3 meters away").

Asking Questions

Voice Commands:

Ask questions regarding the detected objects or the scene. Example questions include:

  • "Hey Vision, what do you see?"
  • "Hey Vision, is there a door in front of me?"
  • "Hey Vision, are there any people around?"

Find Specific Objects:

Say "Hey Vision, find [object name]" (e.g., "find my phone") to locate specific items. The assistant will tell you the object's direction, estimated distance, and characteristics.

Receiving Descriptions

Scene Description:

About once a minute, the assistant gives a short spoken summary of your surroundings, mentioning anything safety-related first and focusing on what has changed.

Emergency Situations:

In an emergency, say "Hey Vision, help." VisuAI will email your emergency contact with your location and a camera snapshot. You have 10 seconds to say "cancel" if it was a mistake. If you are in danger, also call 911.

Additional Features

Voice Feedback:

VisuAI utilizes text-to-speech technology to read out descriptions and instructions, ensuring you stay informed.

Hazard Alerts:

VisuAI watches for cars, bicycles, buses, and other vehicles in your path and immediately warns you when one is close or approaching.

Troubleshooting

  • Microphone Issues: Ensure your device's microphone is not muted and is working correctly.
  • Object Detection Failures: If the application fails to recognize objects, adjust the camera angle or distance, or improve the lighting.
  • Wake Word Not Responding: Make sure the environment is not too noisy, which may interfere with the voice recognition. VisuAI ignores speech while it is talking, so wait until it finishes.
